Warming Waters: NASA MODIS Sea Surface Temperature Explorer

This interactive visualization uses NASA MODIS Aqua and Terra Level-3 monthly sea surface temperature imagery from the NASA GIBS WMS API. Use the controls to compare ocean temperature patterns by region, satellite, time of day, month, and year.

Design Note

The visualization filters NASA MODIS imagery by bounding box, date, satellite platform, and day/night product. Monthly Level-3 imagery is used to make comparisons more stable and less affected by daily cloud gaps.
